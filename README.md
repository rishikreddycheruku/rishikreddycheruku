### 📰 Newsphere — Personalized News Aggregator
`Dec 2024 – Apr 2025`

> A news platform that reads you as much as you read it.

Full MERN-stack app with personalized feeds, secure JWT auth, and a complete admin dashboard. The interesting part: two distinct LLMs under the hood — **Twitter-RoBERTa** for algorithmic comment moderation, and **Mistral-7B-Instruct** powering a conversational AI layer for topic queries. Backend auto-aggregates from **3+ news APIs** across **25+ feed combos** every 3 hours.

`Feb 2025 – Jul 2025`

> Because emergencies don't wait for a signal.

Flutter app built for the worst-case scenario. On-network: **100% delivery success** in Android field tests. Accessibility-first UI (high contrast, large tap targets) — validated by 25+ test users.
